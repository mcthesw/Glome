Open a scene containing an `S3Space3D` and click **S³** at the top of Godot. The viewport shows the curved scene between the Scene tree and Inspector. Click a mesh with **Select** (**Q**) and press **F** to frame it.

![Godot S³ editor with a native toolbar above the curved scene and a shading selector in the viewport.](/media/editor-s3-mainscreen.webp)

## Toolbar and view modes

| Control | Use |
| --- | --- |
| **Select / Move / Rotate / Scale** | Godot 4.7 tool icons. With the viewport focused, press **Q / W / E / R** to switch tools. **Move** is active initially. |
| **Snap** | Toggle snapping for a drag, or hold **Ctrl** temporarily. Translation snaps to 0.5 m of arc, rotation to 15°, and scale factors to 0.1 increments. Hold **Shift** for fine motion. |
| **Anchor** | **New Anchor Here** places an anchor on the ground ahead of the view. Select an editable anchor to use **Duplicate at Antipode**, **Snap to Ground**, or **Face Origin**. These actions support undo. |
| **View** | **Focus Selection (F)** frames the selected node. **Reset View** restores the provider’s starting camera. **From 3D** maps the ordinary 3D editor camera into $S^3$, blending near anchors with `EditorChartRadius > 0`. |
| **View** navigation | **Go to Origin** goes to the origin frame. **Go to Antipode** transports the view to the opposite point. **Level Horizon** levels the camera. **Bookmarks** saves, restores, renames, or deletes a named camera view with its shading mode. |
| **Overlays** | **Anchor Pins** starts enabled. Turn on **Selection Bounds** for projected mesh bounds, **Collision** for the selection’s collision proxies, or **Lights & Cameras** to pick and edit lights and cameras. |
| **Space** | Appears when the edited scene contains multiple spaces. The menu shows scene-relative paths and the closed selector shows the active space’s name. |

The **ViewMode** selector sits in the upper-left corner of the viewport. Switch between `StandardLit`, `ClayClean`, and `Unlit` on the same baked geometry. Host-provided views appear here for the spaces they support. Use **View → Rebake Preview** to request a new bake.

## Navigate and place scenes

| Viewport input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Hold RMB and move the mouse | Look around. Fly with **WASD** and **Q / E**. Hold **Shift** to boost. |
| RMB + wheel | Adjust fly speed between 0.25 and 128 m/s. A temporary readout shows the new speed. **View → Reset Fly Speed** restores 8 m/s. |
| MMB drag / **Shift** + MMB drag | Orbit the pivot / pan the camera and pivot together. |
| Wheel | Dolly along the view direction. |

Drag a 3D `.tscn` from the **FileSystem** dock onto a surface in the preview. The placed node keeps its `PackedScene` link and aligns its local up axis to the surface. With an editable chart selected, the drop lands inside that chart’s forward hemisphere. Clear the selection to place a separate anchor in the space. A dropped scene with an ordinary 3D root gets an anchor wrapper.

In **Select** mode, click the visible geometry to pick its source mesh. A click on a locked scene instance selects its root. Enable **Editable Children** on the instance in the Scene tree to select individual children. Clicking ground clears the selection. **Ctrl+D** duplicates the selected node in place and preserves scene links. Placement and duplication each create one undo action.

## Edit a node on the sphere

Use **W** to move along an axis or in a handle plane, **E** to rotate with the three rings, and **R** to scale an editable mesh or anchor. While dragging, a curved wireframe previews the edit over the last baked scene. Release to commit a single undoable change, or press **Esc** to discard the drag. Anchors store geodesic moves and rotations in `RelativeSpin`. Child nodes store their local transform. If the gizmo lies within 0.02 radians of the view’s antipode, press **F** to reframe it.

## Inspect distortion and scale a scene

The Inspector displays **Farthest vertex**, **Chart angle**, and **Radial compression** on anchors, and great-circle distances on `S3Space3D`.

- Edit **Radius** directly to change curvature while keeping existing metre coordinates intact.
- Use **Scale to → Scale Entire Scene** to scale `Radius` and all metric scene values proportionally by $R_{\text{new}} / R_{\text{old}}$ (undoable; skips nested spaces and viewports).
