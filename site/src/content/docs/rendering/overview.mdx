## Which approach fits your effect?

| What you need | Approach | Set on `S3Space3D` |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Built-in PBR lighting, distance fog, unlit inspection, or clay preview | [Built-in rendering](/rendering/layer-1-builtin/) | Configure `Environment` and scene `StandardMaterial3D` resources |
| Screen-space color grading, posterization, or vignette on the finished image | [Post-processing](/rendering/layer-2-post-process/) | Assign a `ShaderMaterial` to `OutputMaterial` |
| Per-hit distance, surface normals, material IDs, custom shadows, or custom intersections | [Custom compute shader](/rendering/layer-3-compute-shader/) | Assign an `S3RenderProgram` to `RenderProgram` |

Post-processing (`OutputMaterial`) composes on top of either built-in or custom compute rendering.
